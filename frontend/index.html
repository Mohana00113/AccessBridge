About the project

Who does this help?

🤟

Deaf & mute users

Show a hand gesture to the camera. MediaPipe detects your landmarks, the CNN model recognizes the sign, and pyttsx3 speaks it out loud instantly.

🎙️

Blind users

Speak naturally into the microphone. SpeechRecognition converts your voice to text in real-time, displayed clearly on screen for others to read.

🔁

Two-way communication

Switch between sign-to-voice and voice-to-text with one tap — enabling complete conversations for everyone.

Technology

How it works

Five modules working together — gesture to speech in under 100ms.

01
OpenCV

Camera capture

Opens webcam at 30fps, passes each frame to MediaPipe.

02
MediaPipe

Hand detection

Maps 21 landmark points as x, y, z coordinates.

03
TensorFlow

Recognition

CNN model identifies gesture with confidence score.

04
pyttsx3

Speech output

Converts predicted text to natural voice instantly.

05
SpeechRecog

Voice to text

Mic input transcribed in real-time on screen.
